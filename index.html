The event

A ride and run for Chennai's tech community.

Techathlon brings Chennai's tech community together for a ride and a run, followed by short AI talks and breakfast.

As Community Partner, Chennai Cyclists supports us on the ground and with its riders.

Details are at techathlon.fit.

We want Chennai Cyclists as Community Partner because a ride is safer and better with riders who know the road, and no one in Chennai knows the roads, the riders or the culture better than your club.

What Chennai Cyclists gets

In return, the club is front and centre.

Visibility

  • Co-brandingYour logo as Community Partner on techathlon.fit, on the event t-shirt, on banners and the start arch, and on the medal ribbon where practical.
  • CreditA named mention in all event posts and announcements.
  • Stage timeA short welcome slot at flag-off or the tech session to tell the room about the club.
  • National and international exposureVisibility for the club through the BYCS and Fit India networks.

For your riders

  • Volunteer perksA t-shirt, medal and breakfast for every volunteer, plus an e-certificate.
  • Free entriesA set number of complimentary entries for core members. Number to agree
  • Member benefitA discount code and early registration access for your riders.
  • ContentPhotos and video from the day, shared for your own use.

Growth

  • Membership growthA direct route for corporate riders to join Chennai Cyclists after the event, with their consent.
  • Corporate accessIntroductions to tech companies and potential sponsors.
  • Future roleFirst right to continue as Community Partner in later Chennai editions, and a say in route and ride format.
